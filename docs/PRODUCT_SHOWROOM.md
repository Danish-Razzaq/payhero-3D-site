# PayHero — Product showroom (homepage)

**Status:** v3.0 — replaces the abstract “financial world” tunnel.

The homepage is an **interactive 3D product presentation** of the physical card terminal. Scroll orbits, zooms, and reframes **the same machine**; copy and feature callouts attach to real parts of that machine.

---

## Asset constraint (do not invent a model)

| Asset | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `public/machine.jpeg` | Client source photograph of the POS terminal (Visa card in the reader). |
| `public/machine.png` | Cut-out of that photograph (studio backdrop removed). Generated by `scripts/cutout-machine.py`. |

There is **no GLB / GLTF / OBJ**. We must not fabricate keypad geometry, a chassis, or a fake reverse side and call it the product.

**What this enables**

- The real machine as a photographic plane in a lit studio
- Camera dolly / orbit / zoom around it
- Subtle product tilt (kept shallow so the plane never goes edge-on)
- Highlight markers and leader lines on the display, keypad, card reader, and printer
- Scroll-tied information

**What a real 3D model would add (needed from the client)**

- Orbit past ~25° without seeing a flat photo
- Reverse / side / cable ports
- True part isolation (keys depress, printer lid, card travel)

Until a model exists, the photograph is the source of truth.

## Visual rules

- Studio, not a black void: `#e8eef6` floor/wall, white platform, PayHero blue highlights
- The terminal is always the largest, sharpest object
- No floating cubes, grids-as-tunnels, dust storms, or unrelated geometry
- Typography is dark ink on the light stage; callouts are white cards with leader lines
- Mobile: product in the upper half, copy below — not a scaled-down desktop overlay
