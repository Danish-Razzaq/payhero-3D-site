# PayHero — Creative Direction

**Status:** v2.0 — supersedes the “light SaaS + CSS depth” thesis.
**North star:** *An Interactive Financial World.* Not a landing page with effects.

---

## What this is

The visitor is not reading a brochure. They are travelling through PayHero’s product: a merchant statement exists as a physical object in space, is pulled apart into fee layers, compared against a benchmark, and resolved into a savings figure.

If you strip the copy, the page must still read as a 3D product environment. If it can be mistaken for a SaaS template, it has failed.

References for **quality of immersion only** (never layout, colour, type, or assets): [lusion.co](https://lusion.co/), [unseen.co](https://unseen.co/). PayHero keeps its own identity: DM Sans, navy `#0A1628`, brand blue `#2668FF`, savings green `#1FBA72`, US fintech candour.

---

## What this is not

- A normal stacked marketing page with fade-ins
- CSS `translateZ` and shadows pretending to be WebGL
- A dark neon “AI startup” playground
- A copy of Lusion or Unseen
- Spectacle that hides the upload CTA

---

## Visual world

The 3D environment is **PayHero ink** — deep navy space, soft blue key light, one success-green accent on savings. Product UI lives as **physical panels** (rounded slabs with real materials, thickness, and lighting), not screenshots pinned on a quad.

Objects earn their place:

| Object | Why it exists |
| --- | --- |
| Analyzer chassis | The product. First thing the camera sees. |
| Statement card | The artefact the merchant actually has. |
| Fee-layer slabs | Interchange / network / markup — the honest breakdown. |
| Comparison bars | You vs PayHero vs savings, as geometry. |
| Industry tiles | Who we serve, as a spatial constellation. |
| Two pricing slabs | The fork in the path, left and right in depth. |
| Onboarding documents | Trust, stacked then fanned. |
| Data dust (points) | Ambient “processing” — sparse, not a particle carnival. |

---

## Camera is the narrator

The camera never sits still for the whole page. Scroll is the dolly, crane, and orbit. Mouse is a secondary, damped offset — elegant, not a first-person shooter.

Typography is a spatial layer: large headings occupy the left third of the viewport and fade with the scene they belong to. They never cover the object the camera is currently looking at.

---

## Conversion is still the job

Upload Statement and Estimate Manually remain real links with the live `data-cta` values. The 3D world sells the product; it does not trap the merchant. Inner routes (`/upload`, onboarding, blog) stay functional HTML. The homepage is the world.
